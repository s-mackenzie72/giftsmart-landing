Need assistance or have questions about GiftSmart? Browse our FAQs below or contact our support team directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does the AI gift search work?

GiftSmart uses OpenAI to match your contact's profile (likes, dislikes, sizing, relationship context, budget limits) with real, searchable retail items. The more details you provide in the Preferences section of a contact, the more tailored and relevant the recommendations will be.

2. Does GiftSmart upload my address book?

No. When you import contacts, all processing occurs locally on your device. Only the specific contacts you choose to select and import are saved to your account database. We never upload or save your full address book to any external servers.

3. How do I delete my account or contact data?

You can delete recipient profiles and individual occasions directly within the app. If you wish to delete your user account and purge all of your data from our database, send a request to hello@giftsmart.app. We will process your deletion and confirm within 30 days.

4. Why do some store links open in web searches?

When an affiliate link is not pre-populated, the app dynamically constructs a clean search query directing you directly to the retailer's catalog (e.g. Etsy, Myer, The Iconic, Amazon, eBay, Temu). This ensures you get active results rather than dead pages.
