Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Welcome to GiftSmart ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the GiftSmart iOS application and website (giftsmart.app).

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and password when you create an account.
  • Contact information you enter: Names, dates of birth, preferences, and event details for people you add to GiftSmart. This data is stored securely and used solely to provide gift recommendations.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the app, including gift searches performed and affiliate links clicked.
  • Push notification token: To send you event reminders (only if you grant permission).
  • Waitlist email: If you sign up on our website, we collect your email address to notify you of our launch.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the GiftSmart service.
  • To send event reminder push notifications.
  • To generate gift recommendations tailored to your contacts' preferences.
  • To communicate with you about the app (launch updates, support).
  • To analyse aggregate, anonymised usage patterns to improve the product.

3. Affiliate Links & Third-Party Stores

GiftSmart participates in affiliate advertising programs including the Amazon Associates Program, and programs run by Admitad, eBay Partner Network, and Commission Factory. When you click through to a product and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

We do not share your personal data with affiliate partners. When you click a link, you are subject to the privacy policy of the destination website (e.g., Amazon, AliExpress, eBay).

Amazon disclosure: As an Amazon Associate, GiftSmart earns from qualifying purchases.

4. AI-Powered Recommendations

Gift recommendations are generated using an AI language model (OpenAI). When generating suggestions, we send anonymised product information and generalised preference categories (e.g., "likes gardening, budget $50–$100 AUD") to OpenAI's API. We do not send personally identifiable information such as names or contact details to OpenAI.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (Postgres database hosted on AWS). We use row-level security to ensure your data is accessible only to your account. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal data with third parties except:

  • Service providers necessary to operate the app (Supabase, OpenAI, Expo) — bound by their own privacy policies.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.

7. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at hello@giftsmart.app. Deletion will be completed within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

GiftSmart is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify users of material changes via the app or email.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Contact us at hello@giftsmart.app or via our Facebook page.
